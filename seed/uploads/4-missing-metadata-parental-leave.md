# Parental leave — payroll handling

## Purpose
This guidance explains how consultants record parental leave in payroll so that salary, benefits and social security reporting stay correct during the leave.

## Who it applies to
Employees who take full-time or part-time parental leave under their national scheme. The exact entitlement depends on the country and is set by the national scheme, not by this note.

## Rules
- Parental leave must be requested in writing at least two months before the start date.
- During full-time leave, salary is suspended; the national benefit is paid by the public body, not by payroll.
- Part-time leave reduces the working-time percentage; fixed allowances follow the new percentage.

## How to process in payroll
Record the leave with absence code PL-01 before the monthly cut-off, and send the social security declaration in the same month.
