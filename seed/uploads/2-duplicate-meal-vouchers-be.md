# Meal vouchers BE — consultant cheat sheet

## Purpose
A short cheat sheet I keep next to my payroll checklist so I don't have to open the full guidance every month.

## The essentials
- One voucher per day actually worked, never for absence days.
- Face value is at most €10; the employee pays at least €1.09 and the employer at most €8.91.
- Employee share goes on deduction code 9120.

## Key rules
- Max face value: €10 per voucher
- Employee share: minimum €1.09 per voucher
- Employer share: maximum €8.91 per voucher
- Granting basis: one voucher per day actually worked
- Deduction code: 9120
