# Home-office allowance — note forwarded by email

## Purpose
Pieter forwarded this from an email thread with a client HR manager. Saving it so the team doesn't have to dig through the inbox again.

## Summary
- The client asked what the maximum tax-free home-office allowance is for their Belgian employees.
- The email says they can pay €130 per month from September, for employees who work from home at least one day per week.
- Book it on the usual payslip code 1250.

## Key rules
- Monthly cap: €130
- Eligibility: structural home work, at least 1 day per week
- Payslip code: 1250
