# Mobility budget — Belgium

## Purpose
This guidance explains how consultants set up and process the Belgian mobility budget, which lets employees who are entitled to a company car exchange it for a yearly budget spent on sustainable mobility and, as a last resort, cash.

## Who it applies to
Employees on a Belgian payroll who have had, or were eligible for, a company car for at least 12 of the previous 36 months, and whose employer offers the mobility budget in its policy.

## Rules
- The yearly budget equals the total cost of ownership of the company car the employee gives up, with a minimum of €3,000 and, from 1 October 2026, a maximum of €16,500 per year (was €16,000).
- Pillar 1: an environmentally friendly car. Pillar 2: sustainable mobility (public transport, bicycles, housing close to work). Pillar 3: the remaining balance, paid in cash once a year.
- The pillar-3 cash balance is subject to a special social security contribution of 38.07% and is not subject to withholding tax.
- Unused balances cannot be carried over to the next year.

## How to process in payroll
Book pillar-3 payouts in the December payroll on payslip code 2310. Pillar-2 expenses are reimbursed through the mobility provider and do not appear on the payslip. For budgets already capped at €16,000, recalculate before the October cut-off.

## Exceptions
Employees who switch to the budget mid-year receive a pro-rated budget. Contact the policy owner for employees on long-term sickness, as the budget follows the client's company-car policy during suspension.
