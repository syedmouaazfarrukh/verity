# Holiday pay — Belgium (white-collar)

## Purpose
This guidance explains how consultants calculate and pay holiday pay (vakantiegeld / pécule de vacances) for white-collar employees on a Belgian payroll. Blue-collar holiday pay is paid by the holiday fund and is out of scope.

## Who it applies to
White-collar employees with a Belgian employment contract, including part-time employees. Holiday entitlement is based on the days worked in the previous calendar year (the "holiday exercise").

## Rules
- Simple holiday pay: the normal monthly salary continues to be paid during holiday days.
- Double holiday pay: 92% of the gross monthly salary, paid once a year.
- Double holiday pay is paid in May or June, and at the latest when the employee takes their main holiday.
- Leavers receive an exit holiday pay (15.34% of the gross salaries earned in the current and previous year, minus what has already been paid).

## How to process in payroll
Double holiday pay is booked on payslip code 3100 in the May payroll by default. Apply the special withholding-tax rate for exceptional payments, not the monthly scale. For leavers, calculate the exit holiday pay in the final pay run and issue the holiday certificate to the employee.

## Exceptions
Employees who started in the previous year receive a pro-rated double holiday pay. Some clients pay double holiday pay in June by agreement; this is recorded on the client's service sheet.
