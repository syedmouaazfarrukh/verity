# Meal vouchers — Belgium

## Purpose
This guidance explains how consultants set up and process electronic meal vouchers for Belgian client employees so that they remain exempt from social security contributions and withholding tax.

## Who it applies to
All employees on a Belgian payroll whose employer grants meal vouchers under a collective or individual written agreement. Students and flexi-job workers follow the same rules unless the client policy excludes them.

## Rules
- One voucher is granted per day actually worked. Days of holiday, sickness or other absence do not give right to a voucher.
- The maximum face value is €10 per voucher.
- The employee must contribute at least €1.09 per voucher, deducted from net pay.
- The employer contribution may not exceed €8.91 per voucher.
- Vouchers are electronic and valid for 12 months from the date they are loaded.

## How to process in payroll
Count working days from the time registration after the monthly cut-off. Book the employee contribution on deduction code 9120 (net deduction). The employer contribution is not shown as pay. Send the voucher order file to the issuer on the first working day after payroll closure.

## Exceptions
If a client grants vouchers on a fixed monthly basis instead of per day worked, the exemption is lost: escalate to the policy owner before processing. Part-time employees receive vouchers based on hours worked if the client uses the hours-based formula (total hours / 7.6).
