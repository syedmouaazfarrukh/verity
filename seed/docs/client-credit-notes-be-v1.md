# Client credit notes — Belgium

## Purpose
This procedure explains when and how the finance team issues a credit note to a Belgian client, for example after an invoicing error, a disputed payroll service fee or a contract change applied retroactively. It keeps credit notes traceable and makes sure revenue is only reduced with the right approval.

## Who it applies to
Finance administrators and controllers who handle invoicing for Belgian client entities. Payroll consultants do not issue credit notes; they report the underlying error to finance.

## Rules
- Every credit note refers to the original invoice number and states the reason in plain words.
- Credit notes up to €5,000 can be approved by the account's finance administrator.
- Credit notes above €5,000 need approval from the finance controller before they are sent.
- Book credit notes on code CN-400 so they are reported separately from regular revenue corrections.
- Issue the credit note within 5 working days after approval, and never in a closed accounting period.
