# Home-working allowance — Netherlands

## Purpose
This guidance explains how consultants process the untaxed home-working allowance (thuiswerkvergoeding) for Dutch client employees. Unlike Belgium, the Dutch allowance is expressed per home-working day, not as a monthly lump sum.

## Who it applies to
Employees on a Dutch payroll who work from home for all or part of a day under an arrangement agreed with their employer. The allowance can be paid on days worked from home; it cannot be paid on the same day as an untaxed commuting allowance.

## Rules
- The maximum untaxed amount is €2.40 per home-working day for 2026.
- Clients may pay a fixed monthly amount based on a stable working pattern (for example, 2 home days per week), provided it is recalculated if the pattern changes by more than 10%.
- Days of sickness, leave or holidays do not count as home-working days, but a fixed pattern may ignore short absences.
- A day counts as a home-working day or a commuting day, never both.

## How to process in payroll
Use wage component code NL-4120 ("Thuiswerkvergoeding – onbelast"). Amounts above €2.40 per day go to the work-related costs scheme (WKR) free space or are taxed as wages, according to the client's choice. Record the agreed pattern in the employee file so the monthly amount can be checked.

## Exceptions
Employees without a fixed workplace at the employer (e.g. field staff) follow the client's travel policy instead. Contact the policy owner for mixed commuting/home patterns that change weekly.

## Key rules
- Daily allowance: €2.40 per home-working day
- Eligibility: agreed home-working days, not combined with commuting allowance on the same day
- Payslip code: NL-4120
- Taxable excess treatment: WKR free space or taxed as wages
