# Monthly payroll cut-off — all countries

## Purpose
This guidance sets one group-wide cut-off for changes to the monthly payroll, so that consultants in every country give clients the same answer to "when do you need our changes?".

## Who it applies to
All consultants processing monthly payrolls for clients in any country. Weekly and four-weekly payrolls have their own calendars published per client.

## Rules
- All changes for the current month's payroll (new hires, leavers, salary changes, fixed elements, absences) must be received by the 20th of the month at 17:00 CET.
- If the 20th falls on a weekend or public holiday, the cut-off moves to the previous working day.
- Changes received after the cut-off are processed in the next month's payroll as a retroactive correction, unless the client requests an off-cycle run.
- Off-cycle runs are charged according to the client's contract and must be approved by the client's payroll contact in writing.
- Leavers communicated after the cut-off are paid their final salary through an off-cycle run within 5 working days.

## How to process in payroll
Log the date and time each change is received in the client ticket. Tickets received after cut-off must be tagged "next-cycle". Do not reopen a closed payroll run without approval from the team lead.

## Exceptions
Clients with a contractual cut-off different from the 20th keep their own date; this is recorded on the client's service sheet. December payroll uses the 15th because of the holiday period.

## Key rules
- Cut-off day: 20th of the month
- Cut-off time: 17:00 CET
- Weekend rule: moves to the previous working day
- Late changes: processed next month as retroactive correction
- December cut-off day: 15th of the month
