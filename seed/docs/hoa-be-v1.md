# Home-office allowance — Belgium

## Purpose
This guidance explains how consultants set up and process the monthly home-office allowance for Belgian client employees. The allowance is a tax-free reimbursement of costs "proper to the employer" (heating, electricity, small office supplies) for employees who work from home on a structural basis.

## Who it applies to
Employees on a Belgian payroll who work from home structurally and regularly, meaning at least one full day per week (or the equivalent of one fifth of their working time), based on a written home-work agreement or policy at the client.

## Rules
- The allowance is a fixed monthly amount. The maximum that can be paid without social security contributions or withholding tax is €129.48 per month for 2023.
- Part-time employees are entitled to the full cap if they meet the structural-work condition; clients may choose to pro-rate.
- The allowance cannot be combined with a separate reimbursement of the same costs (for example, an internet or electricity refund on expense claims).

## How to process in payroll
Use payslip code 1250 ("Home-office allowance – exempt"). Any amount above the cap must be split and booked on code 1251 as taxable remuneration. The allowance is not paid during months of full absence (e.g. long-term sickness), unless the client policy says otherwise.

## Exceptions
Occasional home working (less than one day per week) does not qualify. Contact the policy owner for clients with collective agreements that set a higher amount.
