# Holiday allowance — Netherlands

## Purpose
This guidance explains how consultants calculate and pay the holiday allowance (vakantiegeld) for employees on a Dutch payroll. The Dutch system works very differently from the Belgian double holiday pay, so do not reuse Belgian percentages.

## Who it applies to
All employees on a Dutch payroll, including part-time and on-call employees. Directors-major shareholders (DGA) are usually excluded; check the client's policy.

## Rules
- The holiday allowance is at least 8% of the gross annual salary, accrued monthly over the reference period June to May.
- It is paid once a year, in May, unless the collective agreement or client policy provides for monthly payment.
- Clients may pay the allowance monthly only if this is agreed in writing (or in the collective agreement).
- Leavers receive the accrued, unpaid allowance with their final salary.

## How to process in payroll
Accrue the allowance monthly on reservation code NL-5200 and pay it on wage component code NL-5210 in the May payroll. The allowance is taxed with the special rate for exceptional payments (bijzondere beloningen). Check the client's collective agreement, as many set a higher percentage or a minimum amount.

## Exceptions
For employees earning more than three times the statutory minimum wage, clients may agree a different arrangement in writing. Contact the policy owner if a collective agreement conflicts with the client's own policy.
