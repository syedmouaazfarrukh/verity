# Home-office allowance — Belgium

## Purpose
This guidance explains how consultants set up and process the monthly home-office allowance for Belgian client employees. The allowance is a tax-free reimbursement of costs "proper to the employer" (heating, electricity, small office supplies) for employees who work from home on a structural basis.

## Who it applies to
Employees on a Belgian payroll who work from home structurally and regularly, meaning at least one full day per week, based on a written home-work agreement or telework policy at the client.

## Rules
- The allowance is a fixed monthly amount. From 1 January 2025 the maximum exempt amount is €140 per month (was €129.48).
- Part-time employees can receive the full cap if they meet the structural-work condition. Pro-rating is optional and must be stated in the client policy.
- The allowance cannot be combined with a separate reimbursement of the same costs through expense claims.
- Clients that pay less than the cap do not need to change anything; only amounts above the cap are affected.

## How to process in payroll
Use payslip code 1250 ("Home-office allowance – exempt"). Any amount above the cap is booked on code 1251 as taxable remuneration. Update the client's fixed-element template in January so the new cap applies from the first payroll of the year.

## Exceptions
Occasional home working (less than one day per week) does not qualify. For clients with a sector agreement that sets a different amount, escalate to the policy owner before changing the payroll setup.
