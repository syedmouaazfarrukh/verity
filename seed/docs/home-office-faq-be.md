# Home-office FAQ (old)

## Purpose
A quick FAQ compiled by consultants in early 2023 to answer recurring client questions about the Belgian home-office allowance. It was saved on a shared drive and never assigned an owner.

## Frequently asked questions

**How much can a client pay tax-free per month?**
Up to €129.48 per month for employees who work from home structurally.

**Does an employee need to work from home every day?**
No. At least one full day per week of structural home work is enough, as long as it is agreed in writing.

**Can we pay it to part-timers?**
Yes, the full amount can be paid, although some clients prefer to pro-rate it.

**Which payslip code do we use?**
Code 1250 for the exempt part. Anything above the cap is taxable on code 1251.

**Can the employee also claim internet costs through expenses?**
Not for the same costs. Check the client's expense policy to avoid double reimbursement.

**Does the amount change every year?**
It is indexed from time to time. Always check the latest guidance before advising a client.

## Key rules
- Monthly cap: €129.48
- Eligibility: structural home work, at least 1 day per week
- Payslip code: 1250
