# Sick-leave reporting — Netherlands

## Purpose
This guidance explains how sickness of Dutch client employees must be reported and processed, and which deadlines apply under the Gatekeeper Improvement Act (Wet verbetering poortwachter). Deadlines differ from Belgium, so always check the employee's payroll country first.

## Who it applies to
All employees on a Dutch payroll, including on-call workers with a fixed-hours presumption. Temporary agency workers follow the agency's own procedure.

## Rules
- The employee must report sick to their manager before 10:00 on the first day of absence (or before the start of the shift if earlier).
- No medical certificate is required; the occupational health service (arbodienst) assesses the absence.
- The employer must report the absence to the occupational health service within 7 calendar days. For absences expected to last long, the problem analysis must be ready by week 6.
- The employer continues to pay at least 70% of salary for up to 104 weeks; most collective agreements top this up to 100% in the first year.
- Long-term absence must be reported to UWV in week 42.

## How to process in payroll
Register the absence with absence code NL-ZK01 in the time module. Apply the continued-pay percentage from the client's collective agreement. Set a reminder for the week-42 UWV notification when the absence passes 6 weeks.

## Exceptions
Pregnancy-related sickness is reported differently (UWV pays through the ZwangerschapsWet route). Contact the policy owner for employees who fall sick during a notice period.

## Key rules
- Notification deadline: before 10:00 on the first day of absence
- Medical certificate deadline: not required
- Occupational health report: within 7 calendar days
- Guaranteed salary period: 104 weeks at minimum 70%
- Absence code: NL-ZK01
